The ARUNA Trophy Ledger is an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies in Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy keeps its official name, description and grade, paired with original step-by-step guidance that cites the community sources behind each location, count or timing claim. A three-pane ledger puts the staged checklist, the open trophy's cited guide and a missables watchlist on one screen, while dedicated Roadmap and Collectibles views show the missable-safe platinum route and every tracked count with its in-game label. Tick trophies as you earn them: progress is saved in the browser, spoiler mode masks unearned story trophies, and Export/Import move a progress file in and out. The app is plain static files with no build step and makes no network requests at runtime.

## Features

- All 55 official trophies with exact names, descriptions, grades and a cited original guide of 120+ words each.
- Three-pane trophy ledger: staged checklist, detailed guide with numbered steps and source chips, and a right rail with roadmap, missables and collectibles.
- Kind filters (Story, Missable, Collectibles, Activities), Hidden/Done/Todo filters, stage filtering and text search.
- Spoiler mode that hides the names and descriptions of unearned story trophies until they are checked off.
- Full numbered platinum route with per-stage goals, hours and missable warnings, plus a live missables watchlist.
- Collectibles tables with required counts, in-game names, linked trophies and the source for each count.
- Progress in localStorage, JSON Export and Import, and a Reset link — all working offline.
